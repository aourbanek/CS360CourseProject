# CS360CourseProject
Info/code about/for my course project in Bradley University's CS 360 Fundamentals of Data Science course. [BU CS Department Course Catalog](https://catalog.bradley.edu/undergraduate/course-descriptions/cs/)
## Project Overview from Course Material
This semester-long project is your opportunity to apply everything you’ve learned in a way that produces something portfolio; something you’d be proud to show to an employer or grad school. You’ll choose your own dataset, apply an appropriate combination of data cleaning, exploration, modeling, and interpretation techniques, and present your results during our final-exam slot.
## Prospective Datasets
I am currently eyeing two datasets from [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/) for use in the project:
1. [Titanic Passengers and Crew Complete List](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aliaamiri/titanic-passengers-and-crew-complete-list); 2,207 rows; simply seemed interesting to me, not sure what I would do with it.
2. [🎬 Massive Rotten Tomatoes Movies & Reviews](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/andrezaza/clapper-massive-rotten-tomatoes-movies-and-reviews?select=rotten_tomatoes_movies.csv); 1,446,425 rows (many movies have multiple critic reviews); project idea: comparing critic and audience reviews by genre, and creating a model to predict the difference between them for a movie not in the training set when given the movie's genre and one of the two reviews.
